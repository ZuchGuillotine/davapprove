# Pierce expansion and Kirkland fee review

Sources checked November 22–23, 2026. This is an **early-bid reference pack**, not an approval or an invoice. No approved design revision is assumed. Source records and calculation parameters are in `bid-assistant/data/citations.json` and `fees.json`; jurisdiction profiles are in `data/regions/`.

## Routing and retrieval

Pierce uses its public tax-parcel polygons and situs address, then intersects municipal boundaries. Mailing names such as Graham, Spanaway, Parkland and South Hill do not establish city permitting authority. Unincorporated parcels use County; mapped cities without a transcribed schedule remain city-routed with quote-needed costs. The pack covers County, Tacoma, Lakewood, Puyallup, University Place, Bonney Lake, Gig Harbor, Fife, Sumner and the Pierce portion of Auburn.

Auburn and the Puyallup Tribe reuse a single source record across county packs using an array-valued `region`. Packs remain immutable, content-addressed, schema/revision checked and cached by the existing loader. The hosted Worker bundles the current county revisions server-side; the standalone loader retrieves and caches a county on demand. The browser receives only parcel-specific results. The address-first beta searches supported counties concurrently and reuses loaded contexts. Pierce FIPS 53053 is registered for the web coverage selector and MCP screening. No permit-history documents, applicant fields, owner fields or OCR are added.

[County GIS catalogue](https://services2.arcgis.com/1UvBaQ5y1ubjUPmd/ArcGIS/rest/services) supplies municipal limits, possible wetlands, streams, landslides, volcanic/lahar hazards, shoreline designations, military boundaries and electrical service territories. These supplement the existing independent FEMA, NWI, tribal-land, DNR, state-road and archaeological screening. Map footprints have **unconfirmed buffers**; regional cells remain unresolved, never certified buildable. Empty inventories are not field clearance.

The tax layer can repeat identical ground polygons for condominium records. The adapter deduplicates those polygons before containment sampling, preserving unique multipart geometry. Empty interior samples and failed/truncated military or municipal checks cannot establish ordinary local fee authority.

## Independent authorities and professionals

- [Tacoma Power territory](https://www.mytpu.org/about-tpu/services/power/about-tacoma-power/) crosses city lines. County electrical service geometry plus [L&I's municipal inspection list](https://www.lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/electrical/electrical-permits-fees-and-inspections/city-electrical-permits-inspections) route Tacoma Power separately from L&I. Missing/conflicting territory stays unresolved.
- [Puyallup Tribe planning](https://www.puyalluptribe-nsn.gov/member-services/general-services/planning-and-land-use/) and [Nisqually permitting](https://www.nisqually-nsn.gov/tribal-services/building/permitting) are independent starting contacts. They are not an exhaustive affected-Tribe list; off-reservation treaty interests and consultation remain project-specific. Mapped tribal or uncertain land status withholds ordinary local permit pricing.
- [Tacoma-Pierce County Health's adopted 2026 schedule](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/WATPCHD/2025/12/15/file_attachments/3496037/2026%20Fee%20Schedule%20-%20Adopted%20%281%29.pdf) prices single-family and ADU septic design/installation separately. Seasonal reviews, soil investigations, private design and construction remain additional.
- [BAI's published Tacoma reconnaissance price](https://www.baiservicesinc.com/InspectorServices) is a preliminary wetland visit only. The undated page references older technical manuals; it supports a market starting price, not current regulatory methodology. Formal delineation/rating/mitigation remains quote-needed, with any reconnaissance credit to be confirmed.
- [Evercrest's 2025–2026 King/Pierce residential experience](https://www.evercrest.us/adu-pricing) supplies a modest geotechnical report benchmark. It is not a quote for complex landslide/lahar investigation, drilling or laboratory testing; those can exceed the range.
- [County's wetland clarification](https://www.piercecountywa.gov/CivicSend/ViewMessage/message/279912) identifies adopted 2025 updates and Category IV buffer treatment. Unrated map polygons do not receive this buffer automatically. An old PDF found under a generic Title 18E search was a **2013 draft** and was excluded.

## Kirkland correction

The official forms directory leads to HTML schedules as well as PDFs. Normal browser retrieval verified [2026 building fees](https://www.kirklandwa.gov/Government/Departments/Planning-and-Building/Building-Services/Building-Services-Fees), [2026 land-use fees](https://www.kirklandwa.gov/Government/Departments/Planning-and-Building/Land-Use-Permit-Fee-Schedule-2026) and [July–December 2026 impact fees](https://www.kirklandwa.gov/files/sharedassets/public/v/1/development-services/pdfs/pw-dev-pdfs/2026-jul-dec-park-schoolfire-and-transportation-impact-fee-schedule.pdf).

The pack now calculates building/plan review/technology, new-home electrical/mechanical/plumbing, conditional energy and trade reviews, planning tiers, critical-area/shoreline reviews, and detached-home impact allowances with the published area caps. Impact fees do not receive the technology percentage. ADUs and replacement homes do not inherit detached-new-home impact amounts. Goat Hill surcharge applicability/base remains quote-needed. Utility-provider charges are not inferred from the city's name. This supersedes the earlier Kirkland quote-only limitation.

## Verification

Regression fixtures cover each city's fee math, Kirkland caps and trade bases, county area-range limits, contingent scope, septic ADU versus single-family fees, shared Auburn records, tribal/military/electrical uncertainty, duplicate condominium geometry, and all project types. Existing screening, geometry, draft/account bridge, search, exports, summary and DOM tests remain in use.

The opt-in `node tests/bid_assistant_regions_live.cjs wa-pierce` checks nine civic parcels across the eight newly transcribed cities and unincorporated Graham using only parcel/situs/boundary fields. Full live screens also verify electrical routing and source failures: one Tacoma run timed out at the independent tribal-land service and correctly withheld local fees; a Graham run returned all layers and county pricing. These are time-specific observations, not a guarantee of upstream availability.
